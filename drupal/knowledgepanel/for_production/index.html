How you become part of KnowledgePanel

01

Confirm your invitation

Enter the code from your letter or confirm your details with the interviewer and find out more.

02

Answer a short questionnaire

A quick set of questions about you and your household, to join the panel. Once your account is set up, you receive your first points as a welcome reward, which you can redeem immediately.

03

Take part in surveys and receive rewards

From time to time, we invite you to surveys. You earn points you can exchange for rewards. You can stop at any time.

Topics you will be asked about

We send you invitations

From time to time, we invite you to take part in surveys on everyday topics (for example: health, work, transport, the cost of living, public services).

How often and how long

On average, you will receive about 2-3 surveys per month, usually 10-15 minutes each.

Your choice, your control

You choose which surveys you want to answer. You can skip any survey or stop taking part at any time.

Your participation helps reflect real life more accurately

By taking part, you help make sure people like you are not left out of important studies.

Representing different realities

Most surveys only hear from people who choose to sign up. That can leave out many groups. In KnowledgePanel, people are chosen at random, so the results better reflect all kinds of people - including those who don't usually take surveys.

Built for reliable insights

Your answers help create reliable statistics used by public organisations, universities and decision makers to understand everyday life accurately. This means your situation and views are counted, not forgotten.

Your data is protected

We take your privacy very seriously. Your answers are used only for research. We comply with data protection laws (such as GDPR) and with the rules of local authorities and professional research codes.

Confidential answers

Your answers are confidential. They are stored securely and kept separate from your name and contact details.

Anonymous results

Results are published in anonymous or grouped form (for example: "X% of people think that...").

No marketing. Ever

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for marketing or advertising.

Voluntary participation

You can skip any survey and stop taking part at any time. You can also ask us to remove your data from the panel.
