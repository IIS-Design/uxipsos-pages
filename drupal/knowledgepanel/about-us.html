About KnowledgePanel

KnowledgePanel is a national survey panel created by Ipsos to better understand the views and experiences of people living in the country today.

Our Mission: Bring Real Voices into Research

KnowledgePanel connects invited people with researchers who want to understand how people really live.

By taking part in surveys, members help create statistics and studies about everyday topics such as health, work, transport, the cost of living and public services. These results help organisations understand people's needs and experiences more accurately.

What makes the KnowledgePanel unique

KnowledgePanel brings together invited members whose answers help research reflect the reality of everyday life.

A community built with purpose

KnowledgePanel is made up of people randomly selected across the country. This helps make sure that different ages, regions and backgrounds are included in the results.

Your perspective adds value

Every participant's answers help us understand the different realities of people and communities. Your experience and opinions help make sure that people like you are not left out.

Together we improve understanding

By sharing their perspectives, members help strengthen studies used by public organisations, universities and decision makers to better understand everyday life and inform important decisions.

About Ipsos

KnowledgePanel is run by Ipsos, an independent research company founded in 1975.

Ipsos carries out public opinion and social research around the world, on topics such as health, work, education, transport and public services.

We work with public organisations, universities, charities, the media and companies to understand what people think and experience.

Ipsos follows strict professional rules and data protection laws. Your answers are used to create statistics and studies, not for marketing or advertising.

Principles we follow in every study

KnowledgePanel operates with clear scientific and ethical principles to ensure research is reliable, inclusive, and responsibly conducted.

Neutral by design

Survey questions are written in a balanced, neutral way, without promoting any political party or point of view. All opinions are treated with respect.

Inclusive by selection

Addresses or telephone numbers are randomly selected across the country. This helps include people with different ages, incomes, regions and backgrounds, not only those who volunteer.

Quality and rigor

Each study follows recognised research standards and is checked for quality, so that the results are as accurate and trustworthy as possible.

Data protection first

Personal information is kept secure and used only for research, in line with data protection laws such as GDPR.

Your data is protected

We take your privacy very seriously. Your answers are used only for research. We comply with data protection laws (such as GDPR) and with the rules of local authorities and professional research codes.

Confidential answers

Your answers are confidential. They are stored securely and kept separate from your name and contact details.

Anonymous results

Results are published in anonymous or grouped form (for example: "X% of people think that...").

No marketing. Ever

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for marketing or advertising.

Voluntary participation

You can skip any survey and stop taking part at any time. You can also ask us to remove your data from the panel.
